## Fixed

- **Both:** Small trackpad movements now accumulate before changing volume, preventing tiny scroll events from rapidly raising or lowering it. Normal mouse-wheel input still uses the selected adjustment step.
- **Both:** Zoom gestures, including Ctrl+wheel, pass through the circular volume control without changing volume or blocking browser zoom.
- **Both:** Volume Slider Options remain usable when browser storage is blocked or saving fails. Selected options and resets stay effective for the current page session, including after switching the slider off and on. Changes that cannot be saved do not survive a page reload.
