## Changed

- **Both:** YouTube and Twitch now use the same proven interaction path for matching slider pointer behavior, click snapping, wheel stepping, shared control markup, and listener cleanup. The controls behave as before, with less risk of the two platforms drifting apart over time.
- **Twitch:** The options menu, its post-close grace period, and keyboard volume adjustments now hold player controls independently. When these interactions overlap, one expiring hold can no longer hide controls that another interaction still needs.
