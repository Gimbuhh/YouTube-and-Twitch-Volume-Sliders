## Changed

- **Both:** YouTube and Twitch now use the same proven interaction path for matching slider pointer behavior, click snapping, wheel stepping, shared control markup, and listener cleanup. The controls behave as before, with less risk of the two platforms drifting apart over time.
- **Twitch:** The options menu, its post-close grace period, and keyboard volume adjustments now hold player controls independently. When these interactions overlap, one expiring hold can no longer hide controls that another interaction still needs.
- **Twitch:** Compact preview players now keep Twitch's native volume controls instead of injecting the custom slider and Slider Options button, leaving room for Clip, settings, and other preview actions.

## Fixed

- **YouTube:** Opening a new video now transitions directly to the custom volume control without briefly showing YouTube's default icon or leaving the volume-control area blank.
- **Twitch:** In Replace native mode, unexpected player-side volume resets are now corrected to the saved volume instead of being stored, so returning from a blocked ad no longer leaves streams at 15%.
- **Twitch:** Startup volume correction now keeps the saved mute state active.
